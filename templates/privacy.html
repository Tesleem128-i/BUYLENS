1. Information we collect

We collect information you give us directly and information generated by using Prism:

  • Account details — full name, email address, password (stored as a salted hash, never in plain text), optional profile picture, and your declared shopping interests, country, and currency.
  • Shopping activity — searches, wishlist items, price alerts, comparisons, scanned photos, and chat messages you send to the AI Assistant, so we can show your history and personalize recommendations.
  • Usage data — which features you use and how often, login timestamps, and coarse technical data like IP address and browser type, used for security (e.g. detecting brute-force login attempts) and to understand which parts of Prism are actually useful.

2. How we use it

We use your information to:

  • Operate your account and the core features of Prism (search, scanning, wishlist, alerts, AI chat, recommendations).
  • Personalize trending items, recommendations, and AI answers based on your declared interests and activity.
  • Send transactional emails — verification codes, password resets, and price-alert notifications.
  • Detect and prevent abuse, fraud, and security threats (e.g. repeated failed logins).
  • Understand feature usage in aggregate, so we can improve the product.

We do not sell your personal information, and we do not use your data to serve third-party ads.

3. Sharing with third parties

We share limited data with the following categories of service providers, only as needed to run Prism:

  • AI providers (for generating search results, recommendations, and chat responses) — receive the text/images you submit to those features, not your account credentials.
  • Transactional email provider — receives your email address and name to deliver verification codes, password resets, and alerts.
  • Payment providers — if you use a paid feature, payment details are handled directly by the payment provider; we don't store your card details.
  • Hosting & database infrastructure — stores your account data securely on our behalf.

We may also disclose information if required by law, or to protect the rights, safety, and security of Prism, our users, or the public.

4. Cookies & sessions

Prism uses a session cookie to keep you signed in, and a security (CSRF) cookie to protect forms from cross-site request forgery. These are functional, not advertising, cookies. We don't use third-party tracking or ad cookies.

5. Security

We apply industry-standard safeguards, including: password hashing (never storing plain-text passwords), parameterized database queries to prevent SQL injection, output encoding/sanitization to prevent cross-site scripting, CSRF tokens on state-changing requests, rate limiting, and automatic lockout after repeated failed login attempts. No system is 100% secure, but we work to keep your data protected and will notify affected users if we become aware of a breach that puts your data at risk, as required by law.

6. Data retention

We keep your account data for as long as your account is active. If you delete your account or clear your data from Settings, we remove your wishlist, history, alerts, achievements, and stats from our active database. Some minimal records (like security logs) may be retained briefly for fraud-prevention and legal-compliance purposes.

7. Your rights & choices

You can access and update most of your information directly in Settings — including your name, interests, country, and currency. You can also permanently delete your account data at any time from Settings. Depending on where you live, you may have additional rights (such as requesting a copy of your data, or objecting to certain processing) — contact us using the details below to exercise them.

8. Children's privacy

Prism is not directed at children under 16, and we don't knowingly collect personal information from them. If you believe a child has created an account, contact us and we'll remove it.

9. Changes to this policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. If we make material changes, we'll update the "Last updated" date above and, where appropriate, notify you in-app or by email.
